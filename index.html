About the flags

Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a free open database. The NOVA score rates processing from 1 (whole food) to 4 (ultra-processed).

Ingredients are also scanned for refined, high-glycemic carbs. This matters because maltodextrin has a glycemic index of roughly 85–105 — higher than table sugar at 65, and comparable to pure glucose at 100 — yet it is not counted as “added sugar” on the nutrition panel. Modified starches behave similarly.

Net carbs subtract fiber from total carbohydrate, which follows the US label convention. Products with EU labels already exclude fiber from carbohydrate, so their net carbs may read low. Figures are estimates, not medical advice.
